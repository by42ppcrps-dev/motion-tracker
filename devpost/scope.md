# Motion Tracker
Paste your board meeting notes; see every motion and what's missing before the minutes go out.

## The Unique Kernel
It knows what a valid motion record needs (who moved, who seconded, how the vote went) and refuses to call the minutes ready until each motion has all three. Generic note summarizers don't check that.

## Who It's For
The volunteer secretary of a 7-person HOA or small nonprofit board. Today she rereads scribbled notes the night after the meeting and emails the president asking "who seconded the fence motion?"

## The Core Loop
Paste notes → see a motion table with red gaps → fix the notes → paste again until every row is green → copy the clean motion summary into the minutes.

## Inspiration & Identity
Calm, paper-like, clerk's ledger. Plain words, no AI hype.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
LumeGrid already does paid meeting minutes for HOAs; secretaries' #1 miss is unrecorded movers/seconders.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Paste a messy sample, see 4 motions with 3 gaps in red (no seconder, no vote, no mover), fix those lines, it turns green and "Ready for minutes" lights up.

## The POC Boundary
Text in, motion table + gap flags + copyable summary out. Runs in a browser, no account, nothing stored.

## Later
Audio upload, roster check of names, export to Word.

## Explicitly Cut
- AI/LLM extraction: rule-based is free, predictable and explainable for a POC.
- Accounts/storage: privacy; notes never leave the request.
